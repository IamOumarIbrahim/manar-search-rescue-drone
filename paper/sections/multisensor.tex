\subsection{Optical Subsystem and Ambient-Light Switching}
To ensure continuous imaging across diurnal cycles, optical sensors transition dynamically based on ambient illuminance $E_{\mathrm{lux}}$ with hysteresis:
\begin{equation}
\text{Mode} = \begin{cases}
\text{RGB + LWIR}, & E_{\mathrm{lux}} > 150\text{ lx} \\
\text{RGB + IR + LWIR}, & 50 \le E_{\mathrm{lux}} \le 150\text{ lx} \\
\text{IR + LWIR}, & E_{\mathrm{lux}} < 50\text{ lx}
\end{cases}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Acoustic Subsystem and Call-and-Listen Cycle}
The acoustic payload incorporates a 4--8 digital MEMS microphone array mounted on vibration-isolated damping brackets. Rotor harmonics are actively suppressed using real-time motor RPM references across the $200\text{ Hz}\text{--}8\text{ kHz}$ processing band.

During hover hold, the system executes a structured $35.4\text{ s}$ call-and-listen cycle:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Acoustic Alert ($8.4\text{ s}$):} Standard SOS buzzer sequence ($3\times$ short $0.4\text{ s}$, $3\times$ long $1.2\text{ s}$, $3\times$ short $0.4\text{ s}$).
    \item \textbf{Voice Prompt ($12.0\text{ s}$):} Spoken instructions in English and Arabic: \emph{``Rescue drone. Shout, whistle, clap, or hit an object now''}.
    \item \textbf{Silent Listening ($15.0\text{ s}$):} Emitters are silenced while the array records ambient signals, evaluating Beamforming Direction of Arrival (DoA) and classifying vocal distress events ($\ge 70\%$ confidence).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Passive RF Sensing and Two-Level Attention}
Passive RF monitoring scans sub-$6\text{ GHz}$ bands (cellular uplinks $600\text{ MHz}\text{--}2.7\text{ GHz}$, sub-$6\text{ 5G}$, Wi-Fi $2.4/5\text{ GHz}$, and Bluetooth $2.4\text{ GHz}$; $>6\text{ GHz}$ excluded) using dual directional antennas:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Level 1 Attention (CFAR Detection):} Activates when at least three qualifying samples exceed the adaptive CFAR threshold within an observation window ($N_{\mathrm{CFAR+}} \ge 3$).
    \item \textbf{Level 2 Attention (Temporal Trend Tracking):} Engages \texttt{Inspect} mode with elevated sampling. The local trend $\Delta S_k = S_k - \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=k-m}^{k-1} S_i$ is classified as $\text{HOT}$ ($\Delta S_k > \epsilon$), $\text{STABLE}$ ($|\Delta S_k| \le \epsilon$), or $\text{COLD}$ ($\Delta S_k < -\epsilon$).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{FMCW Radar Ranging Characteristics}
Operating at a $24\text{ GHz}$ center frequency with sweep bandwidth $B \approx 250\text{ MHz}$, the theoretical range resolution $\Delta R$ is:
\begin{equation}
\Delta R \approx \frac{c}{2B} \approx \frac{3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}}{2 \times 250 \times 10^6\text{ Hz}} \approx 0.60\text{ m}
\end{equation}

Synchronized feature vectors $\mathbf{x}_t \in \mathbb{R}^D$ sampled at $10\text{ Hz}$ across $20\text{--}50$ time steps ($2\text{--}5\text{ s}$ window) are evaluated by a Mamba State Space Model (SSM)~\cite{goodrich2008towards}. Mamba acts strictly as an alert escalation filter (Reject candidate, Continue verification, or Alert operator). Final classification (False positive, Manual continued inspection, or Probable rescuee) is reserved exclusively for the human operator.
